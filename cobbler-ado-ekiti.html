How to find a trusted cobbler in Ado-Ekiti

Hiring a cobbler should not be a gamble. The right person turns up, does the job properly and keeps their word on the price.

What a cobbler can help with

  • Shoe and sandal repair
  • Bag and zip repairs
  • Sole replacement and polishing
  • Custom shoe work

Before you hire anyone

  • Ask to see examples of earlier work, or read reviews from real customers.
  • Agree what the price covers, including parts or materials, before work starts.
  • Keep the payment until you have checked that the job is done properly.
