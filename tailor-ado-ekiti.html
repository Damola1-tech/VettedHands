How to find a trusted tailor in Ado-Ekiti

Everyone has a story about a tailor who missed the deadline or ruined the fabric. A good tailor is worth finding and keeping.

What a tailor can help with

  • Native wear, aso ebi and corporate outfits
  • Alterations and repairs
  • School uniforms and event outfits
  • Custom designs from a photo you show

Before you hire anyone

  • Show a picture of the style you want and agree the date in writing.
  • Ask to see examples of their earlier work.
  • Confirm the price, fabric and number of fittings before you pay.
