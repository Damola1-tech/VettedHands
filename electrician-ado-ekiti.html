How to find a trusted electrician in Ado-Ekiti

A bad electrician is a risk to your home, not just your wallet. Wiring done wrong can burn out appliances or start a fire.

What a electrician can help with

  • Fixing sockets, switches and faulty wiring
  • Installing lights, fans and changeover switches
  • Tracing power trips and short circuits
  • House and shop wiring for new buildings

Before you hire anyone

  • Ask what caused the fault, not only how much the fix costs.
  • Agree what is included, such as cables and fittings, before work starts.
  • Be careful of anyone who asks for the full payment before touching the job.
