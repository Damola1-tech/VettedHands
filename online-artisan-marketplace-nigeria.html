VETTEDHANDS

An online marketplace for verified artisans in Nigeria

Finding a good artisan in Nigeria usually means asking around and hoping. VettedHands is an online marketplace that makes it simpler and safer: electricians, plumbers, tailors, barbers, phone repairers and more, all in one place.

How the marketplace works

  • Find: browse verified artisans with profiles, photos and reviews.
  • Agree: chat in the app and agree the price before any work starts.
  • Pay safely: your payment is made through the app and held safely until you confirm the job is done.
  • Review: rate the work, so the next customer knows who to trust.

For artisans

Get verified, build your profile and reviews, and get paid for finished work without chasing customers.
