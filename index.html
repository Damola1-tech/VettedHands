Book in three steps.

No more asking around and hoping. You see who is verified, agree the price, and stay in control of your money.

  1. Request and agree the price

    Pick a trade, describe the job and chat with the artisan. You both agree the price in the app before anything starts.

  2. Pay through the app

    Your payment goes to VettedHands, not straight to the artisan. It stays held while the work is done.

  3. Confirm, then we release

    When the job is done, you confirm. Only then is the artisan paid. Something wrong? Raise a dispute and we step in.

Built so both sides are protected.
