Policies

These are the rules for using VettedHands. They are written in plain words. VettedHands is run by VETTEDHANDS LIMITED (Nigeria). Questions: vettedhands@gmail.com.

What VettedHands is

VettedHands is an online marketplace for services. Customers find an artisan, agree a price in the app, and pay through the app. The artisan does the work in person. VettedHands does not sell or ship physical goods, and we do not run a warehouse or fulfilment centre. Because of that we have no shipping or product-returns policy. Refunds and cancellations for services are covered below.

Acceptable use policy

Everyone who uses VettedHands agrees to the following.

  • Give true information. Artisans must use their own name, ID and real photos of their own work.
  • Do not offer or ask for anything illegal, dangerous or harmful, including stolen goods, weapons, drugs, adult services, or anything that breaks Nigerian law.
  • Do not harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against anyone, in chat or in reviews.
  • Do not post fake reviews, or pay or pressure someone for a review.
  • Keep chat and payment inside the app. Taking a job off the platform to avoid fees, or asking someone to pay outside the app, can lead to removal. We cannot protect money paid outside the app.
  • Do not share other people’s private details, and do not try to hack, scrape or disrupt the service.
  • Do not create more than one account to get around a suspension or a review.

If these rules are broken we may remove content, pause payouts while we check, suspend or close an account, and report illegal activity to the authorities.

How artisans are checked

Every artisan applies with a photo of a government-issued ID, a phone number and photos of their work. Our team reviews each application by hand and can ask for more proof before approving. Artisans who are registered businesses may also give their CAC details, which our team can verify. We look into every report about an artisan. These checks lower the risk but are not a guarantee of quality.

Payments

The customer pays through the app using Paystack. We never see or store card details. VettedHands holds the payment while the job is done, then pays the artisan’s share to the artisan’s own bank account. Users do not have wallets or balances on VettedHands, and money cannot be moved between users on the platform. An artisan’s first payout, or a payout after a change of bank account, can be held for up to 24 extra hours as a safety check.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Before the job starts: the customer or the artisan can cancel, and the customer gets a full refund.
  • After the job has started: both sides must agree to cancel.
  • If the artisan never starts: a paid booking that is not started within 7 days is cancelled and refunded.
  • When the job is done: the customer confirms and the payment is released to the artisan. If the customer does not respond, the payment is released automatically 48 hours after the artisan marks the job done, unless a problem has been reported.

Refunds go back to the way the customer paid and can take several days to appear.

Disputes

If the customer is not happy with finished work, they tap “Report a problem” instead of confirming. The payment stays on hold. Our team reads the chat, looks at the photos and the agreed price, and may speak to both sides. We then decide to pay the artisan, refund the customer, or split the money fairly, and we tell both sides the result. You can contact us at vettedhands@gmail.com if you think a decision was wrong.

Changes

We may update these policies as VettedHands grows. The version on this page is the current one.
