About VettedHands

Everybody in Nigeria has a story about hiring an artisan: paying upfront and getting ghosted, or half-done work. Artisans have their own story: finishing the job and not getting paid. VettedHands is being built to fix both sides.

What we do

We verify artisans, let customers agree the price in the app, and hold the payment safely until the customer confirms the job is done. If there is a problem, our team reviews it and decides fairly.

Who is behind it

VettedHands was started by Damola Lawal in Ado-Ekiti, Ekiti State, and is starting with customers and artisans around Ekiti State University.
