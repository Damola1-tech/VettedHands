How VettedHands works

For customers

  1. Search for the service you need and compare verified artisans.
  2. Send a request and chat once they accept.
  3. Agree the price in the app and pay securely.
  4. The artisan does the job and sends a photo as proof.
  5. You confirm, and the payment is released to the artisan.

If something goes wrong, report it. Our team reviews the case and can release, refund or split the payment.

For artisans

  1. Apply and get verified.
  2. Receive requests and accept the jobs you want.
  3. The customer pays first, so you start with confidence.
  4. Finish, send proof, and get paid once the customer confirms.
